Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a subscription last?

Every subscription runs for one monthly cycle from the day payment is completed.

Can unused visits be carried forward?

No. Unused visits expire at the end of the cycle and cannot roll over to the next month.

Can I cancel after subscribing?

An already-paid active subscription cannot be cancelled, and subscriptions cannot be paused. You can opt out of the next renewal at any time from your dashboard.

Can I book both visits in the same week?

No. Subscription visits must be at least seven days apart.

Can I choose my stylist?

No. Stylists are assigned by Sisters Lounge to keep scheduling fair and appointments on time.

Can I add extra services to my visit?

Yes — services like dyeing, henna, manicure or trimming can be added to any appointment for an additional fee.

Are consultations included?

No. Consultations are professional sessions charged separately from your subscription.

Is home service available outside Ilorin?

Not yet — home-service plans are currently available within Ilorin only.

Can I manage subscriptions for more than one child?

Yes. You can create a profile for each child, subscribe them to a plan and manage all their bookings from your account.

Can I change my plan?

Yes — plan changes take effect from your next monthly cycle, and weekend bookings are available on all plans.
